How SmartNotes Works

A four-step streamlined academic revision cycle designed for maximum retention.

1

Create or Upload

Write notes directly or drag and drop textbook excerpts, lecture slides, and PDF assignments.

2

Organize by Subject

Categorize under Cloud Computing, DBMS, Networks, AI, Operating Systems, or Data Structures.

3

Generate AI Summaries

Extract crucial concepts, core definitions, and exam cheat sheets powered by AI.
